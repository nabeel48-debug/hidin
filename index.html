Aesthetic Finish

Silk Dual & Tricolor PLA

Extruded with dual pigments that transition dynamically as light hits the surfaces. Silky lustrous sheen with zero painting needed.

12K Ultra-Resolution Resin

Photopolymer resin cured layer-by-layer at 25-micron increments. Eliminates visible layer stepping for museum-grade figurines.

  • • Ideal for: Tabletop Miniatures, Jewelry
  • • Finish: Ultra Smooth & Crisp

Strong, semi-flexible, and temperature-resistant up to 80°C. Resists chemicals, UV sunlight, and heavy mechanical stress.

  • • Finish: Satin Industrial
